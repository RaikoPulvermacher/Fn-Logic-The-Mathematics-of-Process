# Fn-Logic: The Mathematics of Process

> 📄 **[Download full paper as PDF](./Fn-Logic-The-Mathematics-of-Process.pdf)**
> *(Scientific document with abstract, chapters 1–5, citations & full PORL license)*

This repository documents **Fn-Logic** – a fundamental reorientation of physical and digital computational models. Based on the recognition that the universe operates additively rather than through static multiplication, Fn-Logic offers a lossless alternative to conventional Euler-Logic.

## Core Philosophy: Mathematics Is Time
Fn-Logic breaks with the static view of numbers. Every mathematical value is understood as the result of a continuous process of addition.
**Core statement:** The universe does not calculate – it adds in the rhythm of nature.

## Structure of the Documentation

### [file01_Foundations: The Foundations](file01_Foundations.md)
Definition of the additive process chain ($fn_{n-1} + fn_{n-2} = fn_n$). Introduction of the **O-O-E Rhythm** (Odd-Odd-Even) as the engine of symmetry formation. Refutation of infinity and elimination of floating-point errors.

### [file02_Root-Redundancy](file02_Root-Redundancy.md)
Analysis of the energetic inefficiency of root operations ($\sqrt{x}$). Proof of the redundancy of roots in an additive system. Outlook on a processor architecture without a Floating Point Unit (FPU).

### [file03_Conservation-of-Energy](file03_Conservation-of-Energy.md)
Comparison of the Euler Paradox and Fn Integrity. Demonstration that Fn-Logic satisfies the law of conservation of energy 100% through integer addition, while Euler-Logic produces approx. 89% logical friction (waste heat).

### [file04_IT-Logic: Integer Processor](file04_IT-Logic_Integer-Processor.md)
Concept of an integer processor. Introduction of the **Template Theory**: separation between the lossless back-end (atomic addition) and the human perception layer (scaled display). Revolution for AI efficiency and graphics rendering.

### [file05_Experiment: The Vacuum Proof](file05_Experiment.md)
The decisive experiment: Two vacuum drop towers to compare fall distances.
* **Euler mark:** 122.625 m (5.000 s expected / 4.500 s real per Fn)
* **Fn mark:** 127.530 m (**5.000 s real** / 5.099 s expected per Euler)
Proof of the "Euler delay" as the mathematical signature of logical friction.
